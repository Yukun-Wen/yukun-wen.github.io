About

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia — open to collaboration and doctoral supervision.

I work between machine learning and architecture — teaching machines to see, read and draw, then designing airports, hospitals and schools. My research centres on multimodal vision–language models, data augmentation for robust visual question answering, and interpretable AI for biomedical discovery.

Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology, Universiti Malaya.
