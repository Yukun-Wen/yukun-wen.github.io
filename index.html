About

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

I work between architecture and machine learning — designing airports, hospitals and schools, then teaching machines to see, read and draw. My research centres on multimodal vision–language models, data augmentation for robust visual question answering, and interpretable AI for biomedical discovery.

Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology, Universiti Malaya. Open to collaboration on multimodal learning and computational design.
