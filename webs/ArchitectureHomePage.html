01 · Profile

What I actually did

I build public buildings, and the tools that make them buildable. Across four years in two practices I have worked on a terminal, twelve hospitals, five education projects and four houses - as draughtsman, modeller, BIM lead and scheme designer in turn. At Hualan Group I founded the department's BIM team, then worked with its Big Data department on research that joined the two disciplines; the parametric and computational work on this site grew out of that. What I am after sits on the seam between drawing, data and construction, and I try to be fluent on both sides of it.
